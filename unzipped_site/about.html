Precision, Integrity, and Grit

We are Gerald, Cole, Dom, and Gerardo. Years ago, we met working together on a high-end commercial landscaping site. We quickly realized we all shared the same relentless work ethic and passion for perfection. We clicked immediately and came up with the idea to form our own specialized crew—taking on custom hardscape side jobs exclusively on weekends.

Because we all work during the week, The Elite 4 operates 100% on Saturdays and Sundays. We are a specialized four-man strike-force. When you hire us, you are hiring the owners of the company. No subcontractors, no middlemen. Just four guys bringing grit, grace, and unmatched symmetry to your Minnesota landscape over a single weekend.
